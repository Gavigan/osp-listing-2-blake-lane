02 · The Residence

A regional landmark in the making

Iconic by design.
Unforgettable in person.

The Hancock unites landmark architecture with a highly livable, primarily one-level plan.

An open kitchen, dining and Grand Room form the center of the home, while a private primary-suite wing, generous guest accommodations and indoor-outdoor gathering spaces make every generation feel genuinely at home.

01

Architecture with presence

Twelve-foot walls establish exceptional scale across the main level. The Grand Room rises from approximately 14-foot-6-inch walls to a vaulted peak of roughly 22 feet, as described in the current listing. Approved plans control final dimensions.

02

Made for generations

Three main-level bedrooms keep family close, while the fully conditioned suite above the garage provides a private full bath and flexible space for longer stays.

03

Lowcountry, refined

Acme Oyster Cove brick, James Hardie Cobble Stone siding, bronze DP-50-rated windows and layered natural materials create a timeless coastal composition.

Is construction underway?

Yes. Drywall is in progress, as confirmed by the owner September 26, 2026. Follow @bullpointsc for current jobsite photos and videos.

How large is the home?

The current area schedule presents approximately 3,476 conditioned square feet and approximately 4,650 square feet total under roof. The garage is the only enclosed unconditioned portion; covered porches are included under roof but not in conditioned living area. Current construction plans, specifications and the executed purchase agreement control final dimensions.

How is the bedroom plan organized?

The home is marketed with four bedrooms and 3.5 baths. The primarily one-level plan includes a private owner’s wing, guest accommodations and a conditioned suite above the garage.

What can a buyer still personalize?

Rough-in inspections have passed. Ask the project team which selections or upgrades remain feasible at the current stage; changes require written approval and may affect cost and timing.

Is a generator available?

An optional 26 kW Generac whole-home package with automatic transfer-switch equipment is available, subject to final coordination and written approval.

What warranty is included?

The home will be enrolled in the Quality Builders Warranty 10-year new-home limited-warranty program. The agreement delivered at closing controls all terms and procedures.

When is completion anticipated?

Contact the project team for the current completion schedule. Timing remains subject to weather, inspections, utilities, material availability and approved changes.
